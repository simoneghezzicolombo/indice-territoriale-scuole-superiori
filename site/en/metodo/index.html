How to read the territorial school index

The index compares the municipalities covered by the available sources, using outcomes weighted by school track and graduate count. Its comparative reference is 100. Municipal averages do not describe every student or school and do not identify causal effects. Missing observations are not zero. Source snapshots and coverage must be considered when comparing places.
